METHODOLOGY NOTES

Systems and adaptation.

These are end-to-end system comparisons under a common protocol; native model backbones and memory representations may differ.

Four memory paradigms

The evaluated systems span recent-context memory, implicit visual memory, explicit multimodal memory, and generic text memory. Generic text-memory systems receive a shared sequence of 10-second textual observations.

Beyond overall accuracy

The paper also analyzes capability-level accuracy, performance across memory distances, and ingestion and storage efficiency.
